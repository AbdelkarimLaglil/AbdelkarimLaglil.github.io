Abdelkarim Laglil is a PhD student in Social Sciences at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, Spain. His research focuses on migration, social mobilization, political violence, and transitional justice, with a focus on Spain and Germany.

He is currently a visiting scholar at DIW Berlin through the SOEP Visiting Young Scholar Program (VYSP), and a member of the organizing board of [PAPERSS](https://sites.google.com/view/paperss-seminarseries/home), the UC3M seminar series for pre- and post-doctoral empirical research in the social sciences (2026/2027). In 2025/2026 he taught seminars and two guest lectures in Topics in Comparative Politics, in UC3M's double degree in Law and Political Science. His work has been presented at ECPR Joint Sessions, EPSA, IMISCOE, EPSS, and MESS, among other venues.
