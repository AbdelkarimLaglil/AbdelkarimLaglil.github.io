Abdelkarim Laglil is a PhD student in Social Sciences at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, Spain. His research focuses on migration, social mobilization, political violence, and transitional justice, with a focus on Spain and Germany.

He is currently a visiting researcher at DIW Berlin (September–December 2026) through the SOEP Visiting Young Scholar Program (VYSP), and a member of the organizing board of [PAPERSS](https://sites.google.com/view/paperss-seminarseries/home), the UC3M seminar series for pre- and post-doctoral empirical research in the social sciences (2026/2027). In 2025/2026 he was a teaching assistant in Topics in Comparative Politics, in UC3M's double degree in Law and Political Science, where he taught seminars and two guest lectures. His work has been presented at ECPR Joint Sessions, EPSA, IMISCOE, EPSS, MESS, and the SOEP Users Conference, among other venues.
