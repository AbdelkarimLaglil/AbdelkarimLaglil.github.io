I am a PhD student in Social Sciences at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, Spain. My research focuses on migration, social mobilization, political violence, and transitional justice, with a particular focus on Spain and Germany. I am interested in how societies confront legacies of violence and how migrant and other social groups mobilize in response to political and institutional change.

My work takes a comparative perspective, drawing on the Spanish and German contexts to examine how political violence is remembered, contested, and addressed. Feel free to get in touch if you would like to collaborate or exchange ideas on any of these topics.
